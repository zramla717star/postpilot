Features

PostPilot Scheduler is built around one idea: prepare your posts in advance, choose exactly which ones go out, and let the queue handle the timing.

1. Two post types, separate daily quotas

Picture posts and video posts have separate limits, so one type never eats the other type's slots.

  • Picture / carousel: 1 to 35 pictures per post, caption + hashtags, up to 5 per day.
  • Video: 1 video file per post, caption + hashtags, up to 5 per day.
  • That is 10 scheduled slots per day in total, each at a separate time.

2. Prepared, Selected, Skipped — you decide

Every queued item has a status. New items start as Prepared. Change the status any time before the scheduled time.

  • Prepared: saved and waiting, will not post on its own.
  • Selected for upload: this is the only status that posts at its time.
  • Skipped: held back, will not post.

Prepared and Skipped items are never auto-posted.

3. Captions, hashtags and separate times

Each post carries its own caption, its own hashtags and its own time. You can write the caption once, review it, and change it before the post goes out.

4. Your own queue, with its own timer

TikTok's Content Posting API has no scheduled-publish time of its own. PostPilot Scheduler stores the time you choose and triggers the post at that time. The queue runs on a timed check, so a post may run a few minutes late on the free hosting plan.

5. Official TikTok login only, no password stored

After TikTok approval, you connect your TikTok account through TikTok's own OAuth login. PostPilot Scheduler never asks for, stores, or transmits your TikTok password. It does not control your phone or any other app.

6. Dry-run mode until approval

Until TikTok approves this app, PostPilot Scheduler runs in sandbox / dry-run mode. In dry-run mode it only logs what it would post, for example "would post video at …" — nothing is sent to TikTok.
