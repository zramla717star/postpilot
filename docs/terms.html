Terms of Service

PostPilot is a scheduling tool for creators. It lets you prepare picture posts (up to 35 pictures) and video posts (one video), with captions and hashtags, and queue up to 5 video posts per day AND up to 5 picture posts per day at separate times. You choose what to upload: each queued item is Prepared, Selected for upload, or Skipped, and only Selected items are posted at their time.

TikTok approval. Posting to TikTok through PostPilot requires TikTok's approval of this app and your consent through TikTok's own login. Until approval is granted, PostPilot runs in sandbox / dry-run mode and sends nothing to TikTok.

Your content. You are responsible for the pictures, videos, captions, and hashtags you upload. Only upload content you have the right to post, and follow TikTok's rules for the account you connect.

No password storage. PostPilot never asks for, stores, or transmits your TikTok password. Any TikTok connection uses TikTok's OAuth login only.

No guarantees. PostPilot does not guarantee TikTok approval, posting times after approval, reach, or monetisation. Scheduled times are best-effort: the app's own queue triggers posts at the chosen times once the approved connection is live. On the free hosting plan, scheduled checks run on a timed job (every 30 minutes), so a post may run a few minutes late.

Video vs pictures. Video is the Content Posting API's primary flow. The picture / carousel flow is to be tested first after approval, and may behave differently from video posting.

PostPilot is an independent scheduling tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or using the logo of any social-media company.
