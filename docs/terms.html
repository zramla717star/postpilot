Terms of Service

PostPilot Scheduler is a scheduling tool for creators. It lets you prepare picture posts (up to 35 pictures) and video posts (one video), with captions and hashtags, and queue up to 5 video posts per day AND up to 5 picture posts per day at separate times. You choose what to upload: each queued item is Prepared, Selected for upload, or Skipped, and only Selected items are posted at their time.

TikTok approval. Posting to TikTok through PostPilot Scheduler requires TikTok's approval of this app and your consent through TikTok's own login. Until approval is granted, PostPilot Scheduler runs in sandbox / dry-run mode and sends nothing to TikTok.

Your content. You are responsible for the pictures, videos, captions, and hashtags you upload. Only upload content you have the right to post, and follow TikTok's rules for the account you connect.

No password storage. PostPilot Scheduler never asks for, stores, or transmits your TikTok password. Any TikTok connection uses TikTok's OAuth login only.

No guarantees. PostPilot Scheduler does not guarantee TikTok approval, posting times after approval, reach, or monetisation. Scheduled times are best-effort: the app's own queue triggers posts at the chosen times once the approved connection is live. On the free hosting plan, scheduled checks run on a timed job (every 30 minutes), so a post may run a few minutes late.

Video vs pictures. Video is the Content Posting API's primary flow. The picture / carousel flow is to be tested first after approval, and may behave differently from video posting.

Fair use. Do not use PostPilot Scheduler to post content you do not have the right to post, to impersonate another person, or to break TikTok's rules. PostPilot Scheduler may refuse or delete queued posts that break these terms.
