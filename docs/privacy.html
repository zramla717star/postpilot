Privacy Policy

What PostPilot stores: the scheduled posts you create — post type (pictures or video), uploaded picture/video files for scheduling, captions, hashtags, and scheduled times — plus your choice for each post (Prepared, Selected for upload, or Skipped) and the queue status (queued, dry-run-logged, and so on).

TikTok login. When TikTok approval is in place, you may connect your TikTok account through TikTok's own OAuth login. PostPilot never asks for or stores your TikTok password. Any OAuth token issued by TikTok would be stored only to keep your connection working and to refresh it, and only after approval and your consent. In v1 (dry-run mode) no OAuth token is stored, because the login flow is not enabled.

What PostPilot does not do: it does not sell your data, does not read your TikTok password, and does not control your phone or any other app. Nothing is posted to TikTok in dry-run mode.

Deleting your data. You can delete a queued post from the dashboard. To fully remove your scheduled posts, delete all queued posts and, after any future TikTok connection, revoke PostPilot's access in your TikTok settings.

Hosting. In the free model this public website is hosted as a static site (GitHub Pages), and the scheduling check runs as a timed job (GitHub Actions). The host's own privacy terms apply to server logs in addition to this policy. No TikTok password, client key, or client secret is stored on this static site.

PostPilot is an independent scheduling tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or using the logo of any social-media company.
