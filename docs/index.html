PostPilot Scheduler helps creators prepare picture posts and video posts ahead of time, with a caption, hashtags and a time for each post. You can queue up to 5 video posts per day AND 5 picture posts per day, each at a separate time — 10 scheduled slots per day in total.

Every queued item is marked Prepared, Selected for upload, or Skipped. You can change that choice before its scheduled time. Only Selected items are posted at their time. Prepared and Skipped items stay in the queue and are never auto-posted.

Why creators use it

  • Plan ahead: prepare a day's posts in one sitting, instead of posting one by one.
  • Separate times: each post has its own time, so posts do not all go out at once.
  • You stay in control: only the posts you mark Selected are sent. Nothing is posted by surprise.
  • No password sharing: after approval, you connect your TikTok account through TikTok's own OAuth login. Your TikTok password is never typed into, stored by, or sent through PostPilot Scheduler.
  • Official route only: posting uses TikTok's Content Posting API, not screen-tapping or phone control.

How it works, in short

  1. Add pictures or a video, a caption, hashtags, and a time.
  2. The post waits in your queue as Prepared.
  3. Mark it Selected when you want it to go out, or Skipped if you change your mind.
  4. At the scheduled time, PostPilot Scheduler's own queue triggers the post, because TikTok's Content Posting API has no scheduled-publish time of its own.

Important notes

  • Video is TikTok's Content Posting API's primary flow. The picture / carousel flow will be tested first after approval, before relying on it for daily posting.
  • Scheduled times are best-effort. On the free hosting plan, scheduled checks run on a timed job (every 30 minutes), so a post may run a few minutes late.
  • PostPilot Scheduler is an independent scheduling tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or using the logo of any social-media company.
