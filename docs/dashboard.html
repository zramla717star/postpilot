Dashboard preview

This is a sample preview, not a live dashboard and not a login page. It shows what a day's queue looks like in PostPilot Scheduler. No login is needed to view this page, and nothing here posts to TikTok.

Sample queue — one day

TimeTypeCaptionHashtagsStatus
10:00Pictures (3)Morning city walk#travel #citySelected
12:00Pictures (5)Market finds today#market #findsPrepared
18:00Video (1)Evening cooking clip#cookingSelected
20:00Video (1)Night routine#routineSkipped

In this sample, only the two Selected items would go out at their times. The Prepared item stays in the queue, and the Skipped item does not go out.

What the dry-run log looks like

In dry-run mode, before TikTok approval, the scheduler only logs what it would do:

DRY-RUN: would post pictures at 10:00
DRY-RUN: would post video at 18:00
Nothing was sent to TikTok — approval pending.

Daily limits, at a glance

  • Video posts: up to 5 per day, each at a separate time.
  • Picture posts: up to 5 per day, each at a separate time, 1 to 35 pictures per post.
  • A 6th post of the same type on the same day is not accepted, and two posts cannot share the same time.
