FAQ

Is PostPilot Scheduler approved by TikTok yet?

No. TikTok approval is pending, and the app is in review / resubmission. Until approval is granted, PostPilot Scheduler runs in dry-run mode and sends nothing to TikTok.

Do I need a login to use this website?

No. This website has no login page. You can read the home page, features, how it works, dashboard preview, FAQ, Terms and Privacy Policy without any account. A TikTok connection is only needed later, inside the full app, after TikTok approval, and it uses TikTok's own OAuth login.

Does PostPilot Scheduler store my TikTok password?

No. It never asks for, stores, or transmits your TikTok password. Any TikTok connection uses TikTok's OAuth login only.

How many posts can I schedule per day?

Up to 5 video posts per day and up to 5 picture posts per day, each at a separate time. That is 10 slots per day in total.

Will a Prepared post go out on its own?

No. Only posts marked Selected for upload are posted at their time. Prepared and Skipped posts are never auto-posted.

Do picture posts work the same as video posts?

Video is TikTok's Content Posting API's primary flow. The picture / carousel flow will be tested first after approval, before relying on it for daily posting. A picture post can carry 1 to 35 pictures.

Will my post go out at the exact minute I choose?

Scheduled times are best-effort. On the free hosting plan, scheduled checks run on a timed job (every 30 minutes), so a post may run a few minutes late.

What happens if my TikTok connection expires?

From 1 December 2026, TikTok access tokens are valid for 2 hours and refresh tokens for 180 days, with a new refresh token issued on every refresh. PostPilot Scheduler is built to save the latest refresh token and, when a refresh token expires, ask you to reconnect through TikTok's own login again.

Can anyone sign up and use PostPilot Scheduler?

That is the plan after approval: it is a public scheduling tool for creators, not a private tool for one account. Each creator connects their own TikTok account with their own login. The live public sign-up is built after approval, after the first test posts.

Where are the Terms and Privacy Policy?

They are linked from every page on this website, in the navigation at the top and in the footer at the bottom: Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
